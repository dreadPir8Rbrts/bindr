How do live changes work?

Mark sold and Save changes write directly to your online inventory after you sign in. Buyers get current availability on page load; open pages refresh within 30 seconds or when the buyer returns to the tab. If a save fails, the workspace tells you instead of claiming the card was updated.

Download backup saves a copy of your inventory. Photos uploaded here stay in online storage. Keep the original image folders when updating the website design.

Edit listing

1. Add this card’s photos

One card per draft. Add its front, back, and any close-ups. The first photo is the binder cover.

Tips for clear condition photos

Use a plain, dark, matte background to help show edge whitening. Keep all four corners in frame with a little space around them. Move closer if the card looks small; move back if a corner is cut off. Tap to focus and check for glare. Retake any blurry shots.

These are optional tips, not an automatic photo assessment. Your photos are not retouched or given a new background.

Choose several photos at once. Large photos are resized automatically; HEIC photos are converted when supported. No cropping or retouching.
